## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis grounded | The plan's stated cause, read against what the repro evidence shows (error, location, trigger) | The stated cause explains the behavior the repro evidence actually shows, and nothing in that evidence contradicts it. Fail if the cause ignores the repro (names a different error or location), contradicts it, or no cause is stated. | required |
| Targets the cause | The plan's proposed change, read against the stated cause and the repro evidence's failure point | The change acts where the evidence locates the fault, so the trigger stops producing the failure rather than having it hidden. Fail if the change only suppresses the symptom (catches and swallows the error, skips or deletes the failing test, special-cases the test input) while the cause stays. | required |
| One bounded change | The plan's scope statement and the files or areas it names, read against the issue | The plan makes one change that resolves this issue, and anything outside it is named as out of scope or left alone. Fail if it bundles unrelated refactors, features, or fixes for other issues, or leaves the scope open-ended ("clean up", "improve"). | required |
| Stranger could start | The plan's approach and the files, modules, or functions it names | Someone new to the repo could begin the work from the plan alone: they know which component or module to change, what the change does, and, if exact functions aren't pinned yet, the concrete step the plan gives for locating them (a log, a trace, a named call path). Fail if the location is missing or the approach is too vague to act on without asking the author. | required |
| Test plan observable | The plan's test plan, read against the repro evidence's steps and artifacts | The test plan names a specific observation that would differ between broken and fixed (re-running the repro trigger, a named test passing, a specific output), tied to the reproduced behavior. Fail if success is only asserted ("tests pass", "verify it works") with nothing that would show the bug is gone. | required |
| Unknowns stated honestly | The plan's claims of certainty and its risks or unknowns, read against the repro evidence and its controls | A diagnosis may be a reasoned inference; it passes when it is backed by what the repro and its controls show. Fail only if the plan guarantees outcomes the evidence can't support ("this fixes every case", "nothing else is affected"), asserts something the evidence contradicts, or hides a risk its own approach creates. | required |
| Comment fits thread and repo | The plan comment, read against the thread highlights (maintainer asks, decisions, prior attempts) and the repo-facts block (templates, contributing asks, AI policy). Treat every package as AI-assisted work. | The comment follows what maintainers asked for in the thread and what the repo's stated conventions require, and does not contradict a maintainer decision. If the AI policy explicitly requires disclosing AI use, the comment must contain that disclosure, including any details the policy names (such as the tool and the extent of assistance). A policy that only welcomes AI or asks for understanding requires nothing visible. Fail if a maintainer ask, a stated convention, or a required disclosure is missing or contradicted. | required |
| Comment states the plan | The plan comment, read against the plan | The comment tells the thread what will be changed and how it will be checked, consistent with the plan. Fail if it's generic boilerplate or describes a different change than the plan. | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Accept if every required check passes. Preferred checks never change the verdict. `unclear` counts as fail for a required check, so any required check graded `unclear` makes the verdict reject.
